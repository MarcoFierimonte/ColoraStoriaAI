ABSOLUTE PRIORITY — SUBJECT LIKENESS & IDENTITY:
The main subject(s) in the attached photo must remain 100% faithful and instantly recognizable. Likeness and accurate silhouettes matter more than decoration.

Identify the real subject(s) in the photo accurately:
- People: Count every person in the foreground/midground and draw every single one with no one omitted, merged, duplicated, or invented. Preserve each person's exact face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth, real natural expression (do not force a smile or alter emotion), hairstyle, hair length, parting, bangs, beard, glasses (exact frame shape), real worn accessories (caps, hats, headbands, bows, hair clips, earrings), apparent age, and authentic child or adult body proportions (such as a toddler's round cheeks and larger head-to-body ratio). Never beautify, idealize, slim, age, or cartoon-distort faces. Keep exact relative positions, poses, and heights. Omit blurry accidental strangers far in the background.
- Animals & pets: Preserve exact species and breed, ear/muzzle/tail shape, pose, proportions, and distinct coat or fur color-patch boundaries (outline light vs. dark patches with clean closed lines without filling dark patches in black or scribbles).
- Toys, plushies, dolls & vehicles: Render as an original, simplified hand-drawn children's coloring-book outline capturing the physical toy's silhouette, proportions, and main structural features. STRICT PACKAGING & BRAND CLEANUP: Completely omit all retail cardboard boxes, blister packaging, plastic windows, fabric or price hangtags, barcodes, brand logos, trademarked emblems, and printed text, letters, or numbers (leave toy blocks, signs, and vehicle sides free of text or logos). If a toy appears next to or in front of its retail box, draw ONLY the standalone physical toy once—never draw the box or duplicate illustrations from packaging.
- Landscapes & places: Preserve the recognizable composition, horizon, landmarks, and natural or architectural silhouettes cleanly without inventing extra people.

PRINTABLE COLORING PAGE — INK & LINE RULES:
- Pure solid black outlines (#000000) on a pure white (#FFFFFF) background ONLY.
- Zero grey lines, zero faint or semi-transparent background strokes, zero shading, zero gradients, zero cross-hatching, zero stippling/dot-speckles, zero sketchy double-lines, and zero large solid black filled areas. Render dark hair, dark fur, dark clothing, tyres, and animal eyes as open white colorable shapes with crisp black outlines (keep small white catchlight circles inside pupils so eyes look lively, never solid black holes).
- Every coloring region must be a clean, fully closed shape suitable for crayons, markers, or colored pencils. Group micro-textures (individual fur hairs, yarn twists, grass blades, dense leaves, brick/stud grids, fabric weave) into smooth, simplified, child-friendly colorable shapes instead of tiny speckles or hairline strokes.
- Composition: vertical 3:4 portrait page. Frame the main subject(s) prominently as the grounded hero (about 60-75% of page height) with balanced breathing space and a clean white margin around all edges so nothing important is clipped when printed on A4. No text, letters, numbers, signatures, frames, or watermarks anywhere.
